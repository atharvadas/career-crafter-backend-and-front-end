❓ FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Use our AI chatbot — it's available 24/7 on every page.

Is Career Crafter completely free? +
Yes — 100% free. Every feature including the career assessment, personalised roadmaps, skill gap analysis, goal tracker, and AI chatbot is completely free to use. No credit card required.
Is this suitable for freshers and students? +
Absolutely. Career Crafter is built specifically for students, freshers, and anyone feeling lost about their career direction. You don't need any prior work experience to get started.
How is this different from LinkedIn or Naukri? +
Those platforms help you apply for jobs once you know what you want. Career Crafter helps you figure out what you want, build the skills to get there, and then go apply. Think of us as the step that comes before the job board.
What is the career assessment based on? +
Our 40-question assessment is based on three peer-reviewed research frameworks: Holland's RIASEC Vocational Interest Theory (1959), Gardner's Multiple Intelligences Theory (1983), and the O*NET Interest Profiler developed by the US Department of Labor.
Can I change my career path after I start? +
Yes! Your roadmap is fully flexible. You can retake the assessment anytime, explore different career paths, and update your goals whenever your direction changes. There are no lock-ins.
Is my data private and secure? +
Your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage — it never leaves your device or gets sent to any server. Each user's data is stored separately and only accessible to them after logging in.
